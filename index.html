Frequently asked questions

How do I run faster?
Tap in step: hit the button when the shrinking ring meets its edge. Timing matters, tapping speed does not.

My athlete is out of energy.
Energy recovers by itself, one point every two hours. Events, career and the daily challenge do not use energy; only training does.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open Training → Sleep on it → Restore purchases. One-time purchases (Elite Coach) are restored; consumables (energy refills and coins) cannot be restored once used.

I was charged but did not get my item.
Restart the app first. If the item is still missing, email us with the date of purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change the language?
Settings → Language.

How do I delete my data?
All progress is stored only on your device. Settings → Reset save removes it, as does deleting the app.
