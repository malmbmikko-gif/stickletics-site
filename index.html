Frequently asked questions

How do I run faster?
Tap in step: hit the button when the shrinking ring meets its edge. Timing matters, tapping speed does not.

My athlete is out of energy.
Energy recovers by itself, one point every two hours. Events, career and the daily challenge do not use energy; only training does.

Does the game cost anything?
No. The game is free and has no in-app purchases or ads.

How do I change the language?
Settings → Language.

How do I delete my data?
All progress is stored only on your device. Settings → Reset save removes it, as does deleting the app.
