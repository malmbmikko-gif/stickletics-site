Stickletics (Tikkukisat) · Updated 20 November 2026

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

  • No account is required and the game works offline.
  • There are no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Game progress (records, medals, coins, attributes, settings) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you delete the app or choose Settings → Reset save.
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app never receives or stores your payment details.
  • The app is not directed at collecting information from children and collects none from any user.
